# Launch kit

## Store listing (mobile)
- Title under 30 characters, subtitle that states the outcome, not the
  features.
- Description: first two lines carry the pitch; the fold hides the rest.
- Keyword set from the scout research, no invented volume numbers.

## Screenshot set
- Five screens: the core loop first, social proof or progress second.
- Real content in every capture, never placeholder data.
- Caption strip on each: four words, one claim.

## Icon
- Recognizable at 48 pixels. One shape, two colors. No text.

## Landing page
- One screen above the fold: name, one sentence, one action.
- The core loop shown, not described: a capture or a short clip.
- Store badges or the signup, nothing else competing.

## Handoff
Everything lands in a launch/ folder in the repo, listed in REPORT.md.
